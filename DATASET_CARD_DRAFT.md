# Dataset Card Draft — QFR training packs

Training packs generated by this repository are candidate datasets for Québec French language-model adaptation. They are not automatically cleared for public release or commercial training.

## Language and dialect

- Primary language: `fr-CA`
- Focus: Québec French

## Data sources

Sources may include local fixtures, reviewed user exports, public-domain-oriented texts, institutional metadata, modern corpus candidates, and catalogued evaluation resources. Each source must retain license, provenance, review, holdout, commercial-use, and permission metadata.

## Excluded from training

- Holdout-only benchmarks.
- Evaluation-only datasets.
- Rejected records.
- Duplicate exact or normalized text.
- Permission-required records without explicit grant.
- Unknown or prohibited commercial-use records in production-commercial mode.
- Quarantine records.

## Synthetic and derived content

Training examples may be generated through deterministic templates, extractive summaries, QA-from-context construction, lexical normalization, contrast examples, or raw-preservation prompts. Each example should disclose whether it contains synthetic or pipeline-generated content.

## Recommended metadata fields

- `source_id`
- `source_family`
- `source_record_id`
- `license_status`
- `commercial_use`
- `holdout_only`
- `requires_review`
- `synthetic_component`
- `generated_by_pipeline`
- `generation_method`
- `human_verified`
- `provenance`

## Intended use

- Local research and pilot LoRA/QLoRA experiments.
- Québec French linguistic evaluation.
- Governance pipeline validation.

## Not intended for

- Public dataset release without source/license review.
- Commercial model training without production-commercial audit.
- Training on benchmark holdouts.
- Claims of exhaustive Québec French linguistic coverage.

## Known limitations

- Corpus diversity may be limited.
- Generated examples may amplify template biases.
- License classification may require manual legal review.
- PII detection from upstream sources may be incomplete.
